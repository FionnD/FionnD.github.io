Topic 4

Evening plans

Use this after work when deciding on dinner, rest, or something to watch.

Start here

Cad a dhéanfaimid anocht?

What will we do tonight?

kad uh YAYN-fuh-mid uh-NOKHT?

Tiny dialogue
ATá ocras orm.I am hungry.
BDéanfaimid dinnéar simplí.We will make a simple dinner.
ATá mé tuirseach.I am tired.
BAn bhféachfaimid ar scannán?Will we watch a film?

Talk about it
  1. Say whether you are hungry or tired.
  2. Choose dinner, a film, a book, or sleep.
  3. Repeat the final decision once in Irish and once in English.
