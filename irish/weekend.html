Topic 5

Weekend ideas

Use this when making loose plans without turning it into a grammar exercise.

Start here

Cad ba mhaith leat a dhéanamh ag an deireadh seachtaine?

What would you like to do at the weekend?

kad buh wah lat uh YAYN-uv egg un DER-uh SHAKH-tin-eh?

Tiny dialogue
ACad ba mhaith leat a dhéanamh?What would you like to do?
BBa mhaith liom dul chuig bialann.I would like to go to a restaurant.
AAr mhaith leat bualadh le cairde?Would you like to meet friends?
BB'fhéidir. Nó fanfaimid sa bhaile.Maybe. Or we will stay at home.

Talk about it
  1. Each person suggests one plan.
  2. Answer with yes, no, or maybe.
  3. End with one real plan, even if the plan is staying home.
