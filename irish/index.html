Gaeilge together

Irish practice topics

Short prompts for low-pressure chats at home. Pick one topic, say the starter line twice, then use the vocabulary to make the conversation your own.

Topics
Morning check-in Sleep, mood, and the plan for today. Coffee break Offer tea or coffee, ask what someone wants. Going outside Weather, walks, coats, and where to go. Evening plans Dinner, tiredness, films, and rest. Weekend ideas Trips, friends, restaurants, and staying home.
Tiny routine
  1. One person reads the Irish line slowly.
  2. The other person repeats it and guesses the English.
  3. Use two vocabulary words in a real sentence about your day.
  4. Click a listen link when the written pronunciation feels too rough.

Pronunciation links point to Teanglann, Foras na Gaeilge's pronunciation database. The written pronunciation is only a quick practice guide.
