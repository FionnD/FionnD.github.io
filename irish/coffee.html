Topic 2

Coffee break

Use this while making coffee, tea, or a small snack.

Start here

Ar mhaith leat caife nó tae?

Would you like coffee or tea?

er wah lat KA-fuh noh tay?

Tiny dialogue
AAr mhaith leat caife?Would you like coffee?
BBa mhaith liom caife, le do thoil.I would like coffee, please.
ALe bainne?With milk?
BLe bainne. Go raibh maith agat.With milk. Thank you.

Talk about it
  1. Offer one drink in Irish.
  2. Answer with Ba mhaith liom... and the thing you want.
  3. Use Go raibh maith agat before you switch back to English.
