Topic 3

Going outside

Use this before a walk, a drive, or a quick look at the weather.

Start here

Ar mhaith leat dul ag siúl?

Would you like to go for a walk?

er wah lat dul egg shool?

Tiny dialogue
ACén chaoi a bhfuil an aimsir?How is the weather?
BTá sé fuar, ach tá an ghrian ann.It is cold, but the sun is there.
AAr mhaith leat dul ag siúl?Would you like to go for a walk?
BBa mhaith. Tabharfaidh mé cóta liom.I would. I will bring a coat with me.

Talk about it
  1. Name the weather in one word.
  2. Ask whether you should go for a walk.
  3. Choose one thing to bring: coat, water, keys, or coffee.
