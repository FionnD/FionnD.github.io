Topic 1

Morning check-in

Use this over breakfast or while making the first cup of the day.

Start here

Maidin mhaith. Conas atá tú ar maidin?

Good morning. How are you this morning?

MAH-jin wah. KUN-us ah-TAW too air MAH-jin?

Tiny dialogue
AMaidin mhaith.Good morning.
BAr chodail tú go maith?Did you sleep well?
AChodail. Tá mé go maith.I did. I am good.
BCad ba mhaith leat a dhéanamh inniu?What would you like to do today?
